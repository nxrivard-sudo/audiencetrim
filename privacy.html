Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-04

Your files stay on your device. AudienceTrim is a static web page. Files you select are read and analysed by JavaScript in your browser. They aren't uploaded, stored or sent to us or anyone else. We have no server that receives contact data.

License key. If you enter a license key, it's checked offline in your browser and saved in your browser's local storage so the tool stays unlocked. It never leaves your device.

Payments. Purchases are processed by Stripe. Stripe receives the details you enter at checkout (for example your card and email) under Stripe's privacy policy. We receive the order record and your email so we can support you. We don't sell it or use it for marketing without your consent.

Hosting and analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement). We use GoatCounter, a cookie-free analytics service, to count page views and anonymous events (for example that an audit was run, with a rough list-size band). File contents are never sent.
