How to archive in Mailchimp
  1. Back up first: keep your original export ZIPs. Archiving is reversible (Mailchimp lets you unarchive), but deleting is not. AudienceTrim never deletes anything.
  2. Unsubscribed or non-subscribed contacts: in each audience, create a segment for Email marketing status is unsubscribed (or non-subscribed), select all, then Actions → Archive. The archive files list exactly who that affects.
  3. Cross-audience duplicates: in the audience named in the file, import each archive__… file for that audience with only the Email column mapped, the status shown in the file name, tag audiencetrim-archive, and "Update any existing contacts" ticked. This only tags existing contacts. Then segment by the tag, select all, then Archive.
  4. Or merge everything into one audience (Mailchimp's recommended setup): import combined_primary_audience.csv into your primary audience with the TAGS column, following Mailchimp's combine audiences guide.
  5. Archived contacts stop counting toward your bill from the next billing period. Mailchimp doesn't lower a peak already reached in the current period (source).

FAQ

Is my data uploaded? No. AudienceTrim is a static page; your files are parsed by JavaScript on your device. There's no server, no account and no Mailchimp login. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

Does Mailchimp really charge for unsubscribed contacts? On current plans, yes: subscribed, unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts count, and archived or cleaned ones don't (Mailchimp Help). Some legacy plans differ; check your Billing page.

How is the saving estimated? If you enter your current bill, we scale it by the share of billed contacts you can archive. Mailchimp prices in tiers, so the real saving depends on which tier you land in. Confirm on your Mailchimp Billing page.

Will archiving lose data? Archived contacts keep their data and can be unarchived (Mailchimp Help); see how to archive in bulk. If you use unsubscribed contacts for postcards or ads, keep them: untick "archive unsubscribed".

How do I get my key? After checkout you're redirected to a page that shows your key and saves it in this browser. Keep a copy.

Refunds? If the audit finds nothing to archive, email us within 14 days for a refund.
