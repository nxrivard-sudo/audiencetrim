Mailchimp non-subscribed contacts: do they count toward your bill?

Short answer: yes. Mailchimp says non-subscribed contacts "are included in your contact total and count towards your monthly contact limit" (About Non-Subscribed Contacts).

What a non-subscribed contact is

Someone who gave you an email address or phone number but didn't opt in to marketing, for example by placing an online order or asking you a question. Mailchimp lists the common sources as a connected online store, an integration, a manual import, and emails forwarded to your Mailchimp Inbox (source). Stores that sync every customer can add a lot of them over time.

Should you archive them?

Mailchimp's own guidance: "If you only use your Mailchimp account for bulk email sending, you may find more value in archiving your segment of non-subscribed contacts. With a lower contact total, you might be able to downgrade to a lower pricing tier." (source)

Keep them if you use them. Mailchimp suggests including non-subscribed contacts in Google remarketing ad audiences, and they can receive transactional emails such as order notifications (source). Archived contacts can't receive anything (About Your Contacts), so archiving gives those uses up for the archived people.

How to find and archive them

  1. Click Audience and pick the audience.
  2. Click the Subscription status drop-down, then check Email non-subscribed and SMS non-subscribed. Save the segment, and export it to CSV as a backup (source).
  3. Select all contacts in the segment, then Actions → Archive and type ARCHIVE (source). Full walkthrough: archive contacts in bulk.
  4. Repeat in each audience.

Note that a contact unarchives themselves if they interact with your connected online store again (source), so repeat customers may reappear.

FAQ

Do non-subscribed contacts count toward my Mailchimp bill? Yes. Mailchimp includes non-subscribed contacts in your contact total and counts them toward your monthly contact limit.

Can I send marketing emails to non-subscribed contacts? No. Mailchimp lists ads and transactional emails as what non-subscribed contacts can receive, not marketing emails or marketing texts.

Do archived contacts count toward my Mailchimp bill? No. Archived contacts don't count toward your contact limit, and they can't receive anything while archived.
